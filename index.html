Ask us directly

What does JPM Media Space do?

JPM Media Space is a creative technology studio in Thailand. We design and build interactive games, touchscreen applications, and interactive content for LED walls, projectors, and sensors, mainly for event booths and brand activations, as well as permanent installations.

How much does an interactive game for an event booth cost?

Event packages start at 30,000.- for touchscreen experiences and 56,000.- for interactive LED and projection content. The final price depends on the game, content, and scope, and is confirmed in your quotation.

Can you use the screen we already have?

Yes. Our content and software run on your touchscreen, LED wall, or projector. Screens and projectors are not included in our packages, so share the size and resolution and we will design for them.

What if something goes wrong during the event?

We test the full system on site before the event opens, and our technical team stays on site from start to finish to fix issues on the spot.

Can the game collect player information?

Yes. We can add a sign-up form or QR scan before play, with a consent step you approve. After the event, you receive a summary of players and engagement to plan your next activation.

How long does it take to build a booth game?

It depends on the game and content. Share your event date when you request a quote, and we will confirm a schedule that leaves time for prototypes and testing before show day.

Do you work with event agencies and organizers?

Yes. We work directly with brands and as the interactive team for event agencies, production houses, and LED providers.
