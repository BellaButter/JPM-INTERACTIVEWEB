## Shared usage across the redesign

Other redesigned pages follow the Homepage icon system: filled Bolt for exploring work, filled Bulb for demos and experience concepts, filled Circle Caret Right for playable demos, Send for quote actions, filled Message Circle for conversations, and External Link for service and article links. Secondary text-link pills are icon-free. Contact details reuse filled Mail, Phone, and Message Circle. Buttons use 15.4px icons with 16.8px gaps on desktop and 10.8px gaps on mobile; smaller card links use 14px icons. Original archived pages retain their historical layout.
