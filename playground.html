SENSOR TECHNOLOGIES / RFID

Place a product.
Its story appears.

Each product has its own RFID tag. When you place it on a reader, the system identifies that product and shows its information on the screen.

  1. Pick a tagged product.
  2. Place it on the RFID reader.
  3. The screen shows that product’s story.

In a showroom or exhibition, visitors can pick up a real product and place it on a display stand to explore more.

This browser demo simulates the product, tag, and reader. A real installation connects physical RFID hardware to the screen.
