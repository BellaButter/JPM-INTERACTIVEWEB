What is an interactive wall (experience wall), and what kind of event suits one?

An interactive wall is a large screen or a projection on a wall that responds when people walk past, stand in front of it, or raise a hand. The image moves with them straight away, so people walking past stop to look, and people who stop want to take a photo home. It suits booths that want to stand out in the hall, and permanent spaces such as lobbies and showrooms.

JPM Media Space·Published 6 November 2026·3 min read

How it works

An interactive wall has three parts: an LED screen or projector that shows the image, a sensor that reads where people are or how they move, such as LiDAR or a depth camera, and the content and software that turn that movement into visuals. JPM Media Space builds the content and software, and it runs on the screen or projector you already have. Our LED and projection packages do not include screens or projectors.

At an event or as a permanent installation

At an event, an interactive wall is a backdrop people want to step into, play with, and photograph. Event packages for LED and projection content start at 56,000.-, and permanent installations start at 160,000.- with a 1-year warranty, and are designed so you can change the content for each season.

FAQ

Do we need a special screen?

No. We make the content to fit the LED screen or projector you have, and we choose the sensor to suit the space.

Can we change what is on the wall?

Yes. Permanent installations are designed so you can change the content for each season or campaign.

Is there a warranty?

Permanent installations come with a 1-year warranty. The scope is set out in your quotation.
