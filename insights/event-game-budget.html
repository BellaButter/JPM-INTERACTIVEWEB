What budget do you need for a booth game?

JPM Media Space event packages start at 30,000.- for touchscreen experiences and 56,000.- for LED and projection content. The final price depends on what you need. This article explains what goes into the budget, what moves the price, and where you can save without losing what matters.

JPM Media Space·Published 30 October 2026·4 min read

What goes into a booth game budget

Part of the workExamplesIncluded in our work?
The game and its rulesHow to play, scoring, start and end screensYes
Visuals, sound, and brand contentLogo, colours, products, questions, campaign messagesYes
Prototype and testingOne prototype version, tested before the event opensYes
On-site support on show dayA technical team on site from opening to closeYes
Sign-up or QR and a player summaryA form before play, PDPA consent, a summary after the eventAdd-on service
Screens, projectors, computersTouchscreens, LED walls, standsNot included; the game runs on your screens or the organizer’s

Splitting it this way shows at once which parts to budget with us and which to discuss with the organizer or screen rental company. If several parties are involved, we coordinate with the screen team, because we already work with agencies and LED providers.

What makes the budget go up

  • A completely new set of rules instead of adapting an existing game
  • Several languages, screens, or modes because each set needs its own design and testing
  • Motion sensors instead of touch
  • Sending data to another system such as your brand’s CRM
  • Content that arrives late or changes after work starts which means redoing visuals and testing again

Where you can save

Keep what makes people want to play and remember your brand: the core rules and your brand visuals. The first things to cut are extra scenes, special effects, or system connections you do not need yet. A hypothetical example: one quiz set done well often works better than several modes with thin content.

Tell us your budget range from the start. We will propose the option that works best within it, instead of proposing everything and then cutting back.

Invest once, keep using it

The system we deliver can be updated, moved, or expanded. If you exhibit at several events a year, tell us when we first talk, and we will design it so the content is easy to change for the next event. Permanent installations come with a 1-year warranty.

Compare quotes fairly

Send the same brief to every supplier, then look at four things besides the total: is there a prototype to try first, when is it tested on the real screen, who is on site if the game has a problem, and can the game be reused. A cheaper quote with no one on site can cost more on the day the game freezes.

FAQ

What is the starting price?

30,000.- for touchscreen experiences and 56,000.- for LED and projection content. The final price is set in your quotation, based on the agreed scope.

Are screens included?

No. The game runs on the screens you or the organizer have.

Should we tell you our budget first?

Yes. That way we can propose the best value within it from the first round.
