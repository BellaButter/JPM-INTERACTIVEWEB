EVENT BOOTH GAMES · INSPIRATION

Designing for participation, not just presentation

A studio perspective on connecting people, brands, and physical spaces.

JPM Media Space·Published 27 October 2026·3 min read

Start with the person in the space

At JPM Media Space, we believe technology should be more than a display tool. An interactive experience invites a visitor to do something: touch a screen, move through a space, or explore a story. The first question is what we want that person to experience.

Choose an interaction with a purpose

Touchscreen catalogs invite visitors to explore information. Games create an opportunity to participate. Gesture-driven displays connect motion to a digital response. Each format serves a different idea; the interaction should make the story easier to explore.

Design the whole experience

Visual content, custom software, displays, and sensors need to work together. Our workflow begins with strategy and system planning, then moves through development, testing, installation, and support. The physical space is part of the design from the beginning.
