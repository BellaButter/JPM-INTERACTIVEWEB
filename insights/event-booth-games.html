ARTICLE TOPICEvent booth games:
a guide to choosing the right game.

A good booth game starts with one question: what do you want visitors to do and remember? Then choose a way to play that people understand in seconds, that ends fast enough to keep the queue moving, and that fits your actual booth. This guide takes you from choosing a game to show day.

Start with a goal, not a game name

The right game for your brand may not be the one drawing a crowd at someone else’s booth. Pick one main goal first, and the right game becomes clear.

Passers-by should get it in seconds

In an event hall, people decide very quickly whether to stop or walk on. A good game shows how to play at a glance, without long rules, and the people watching enjoy it too.

Short, clearly timed rounds keep the queue moving. When you estimate the queue, count the time to explain the rules, change players, and hand out prizes, not just the time in the game. A hypothetical example: 45 seconds of play plus 15 seconds to change players makes one minute per round, so one screen can take at most 60 rounds an hour.

Screens, space, and controls

The same game can run on a touchscreen, an LED wall, or a projector, but the experience is very different. Touchscreens suit small booths and games where players choose answers. Motion sensors need standing space and suitable lighting, but they draw a bigger crowd.

JPM Media Space games run on the screens you already have. Share the screen size, resolution, and booth layout, and we design for the distance people actually stand from the screen.

More than footfall: leads and a summary

A game is the best moment to ask for details, because people already want to play. We can add a sign-up form or QR scan before play, with the PDPA consent step you approve. After the event, you get a summary of players and engagement to plan your next one.

Budget, brief, and timing

Once you have a game in mind, the next questions are budget and time. Event packages start at 30,000.- for touchscreen experiences, and a game takes at least one week to make, depending on the game and content. A complete brief from the start makes the quotation accurate and gets work started sooner.

FAQ

Which game suits our brand?

Start with what you want people to remember, then compare it with the table above. Not sure yet? Send us your event theme and we’ll help you choose.

Do we need our own screen?

Yes. Packages do not include screens or projectors, but our games run on the screens you or the organizer already have.

Can the game collect player details?

Yes, with a sign-up form or QR scan before play and a PDPA consent step.
