JPM Media Space designs and builds interactive games for events, product launches, and brand activations. Games people understand at a glance, finish quickly, and that make people watching want a turn. One team handles it all, from concept to handover.

START WITH YOUR GOAL

Games that start from what your brand wants.

We start with one question: when the game ends, what do you want players to remember or do next? Some events want people to discover a new product, some want a list of interested visitors, and some simply want the liveliest booth in the hall. The answer lets us choose the game format, the rules, and the pace of play.

Every game is designed for your brand: your logo, colours, products that become in-game items, and campaign messages. You don’t need a game idea first. Tell us your goal and your audience, and we’ll take it from there.

Ask us directly

Can you put our logo and products in the game?

Yes. Every game is built around your brand: your logo, colours, products, and campaign message.

Are screens or projectors included?

No. Our games run on the touchscreen, LED wall, or projector you have. Share the screen size and resolution, and we design to fit.

How long does a booth game take to make?

At least one week, depending on the game and content. Share your event date when you request a quote, and we will plan time for you to try the prototype before the event.

What is the starting price?

Event packages start at 30,000.- for touchscreen experiences and 56,000.- for LED and projection content. The final price is set in your quotation, based on the agreed scope.
